# 0080. Channel categories in the sidebar

- Status: Accepted (built: the server, the shared client, the web and the phone app). Amended 2026-10-30: §6 and §7 (channels take an order in a workspace with no categories too), and §6 again (a category with no channel someone can see doesn't show for them, unless they manage channels)
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Code: `packages/engine/src/channel-categories.ts`, `packages/engine/src/channels.ts` (`createChannel`, `listChannels`), `packages/db/src/schema/chat.ts` (`channel_categories`, `channels.category_id`, `channels.position`), `apps/server/src/routes/app/channels.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/Sidebar.tsx`, `apps/web/src/components/ChannelCategoryDialogs.tsx`, `apps/mobile/src/features/categories/*`, `apps/mobile/src/features/chats/logic.ts` (`homeLayout`)

## Context

With a dozen channels, one alphabetical "Canais" list mixes every team's channels. People asked to group them: named sections in the sidebar that open and close.

## Decision

1. **The organization's layout.** A category belongs to the organization (`channel_categories`, with `org_id`) and everyone sees the same sidebar. Its name is kept as typed, at most 40 characters, unique per organization ignoring case (the engine checks the case; the table's unique key is the name as typed). Opening and closing a category is each person's own, in their browser, like the sidebar's other sections.
2. **Who arranges it.** Admins (`configure`) create, rename, reorder and delete categories. Whoever may change a channel (its creator or an admin, as for renaming it) puts it in a category or moves it. Whoever creates a channel may put it in an existing category as they create it.
3. **Order.** `channel_categories.position` orders the categories; `channels.position` orders the channels inside theirs, then by name. Channels with no category (`category_id` null) come first, with no header. Moves renumber the whole category in one transaction (`placeChannel`, `placeChannelCategory`). A group nobody arranged by hand has every position at 0 and reads by name, so a new channel joins it at 0 and the list stays alphabetical, as before categories; once arranged, a new channel goes to its end.
4. **Deleting a category** moves its channels to the end of the ones with none, in their order. Archiving a channel leaves its place as it is.
5. **Live.** Every change sends an org broadcast `channels.layout` with no names in it (private channels stay private); clients refetch the channel list and the categories.
6. **The sidebar.** A category is a section like Agentes: same header, chevron and a + to create a channel in it. Closed, it still shows the open channel and the unread ones. Channels move from their right-click menu ("Mover para…"), the channel details (Categoria) or by dragging. *Amended 2026-10-30:* dragging works with no categories too. In a workspace that has none, the channels drag above or below each other inside "Canais", for the same people, and land through the same `placeChannel` (category null). Before, only a workspace with categories could drag, and with none the list stayed by name. *Amended 2026-10-30 (what each person sees):* someone who doesn't manage channels sees a category only while it holds a channel they can see (the public ones and the private ones they are in, as `listChannels` gives them). A category of private channels they're not in no longer shows them an empty header, and with no category left their channels show under "Canais", as in a workspace with none. Who manages channels (`manage_channels`, ADR 0085) sees every category, empty ones too, to drop channels in. The places that pick a category ("Mover para…", the channel details, Criar canal) still list all of them. The phone's Conversas already leaves out a section with no rows. *Amended 2026-10-31 (the web's look):* a category no longer shares Agentes' header. Lines set the sidebar's parts apart (the tools, the channels, the agents, the people), and Agentes, Mensagens diretas and Canais (with no categories) are titled in small capitals with their chevron after the title, so none reads as a category. A category keeps its header, and its channels line up with the ones in none: every row's mark (an icon, a channel's "#", a face, a category's chevron) sits in one column and every name in the next. A channel's "#" has the chevron's size and ink.
7. **The phone.** Conversas shows the same layout: Canais (no category), one section per category in order, each its five latest and "Ver todos", a closed category keeping its unread channels. The channel's ⋯ sheet and a long press on its row open "Categoria", a pushed screen to pick one; a long press on a category's header gives admins Rename, Categories and New category, all pushed screens. The order moves one step at a time (Move up, Move down): the app has no drag-to-reorder list. *Amended 2026-10-30:* the channel's "Categoria" screen also has its place in the list ("Lugar na lista"): Move up and Move down among the channels of its category, or the ones with none, and where it sits ("2º de 5."), so the phone orders channels with no categories too. The home still lists each section's channels by latest activity, so that order shows in the web's sidebar.
