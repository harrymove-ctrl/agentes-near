## Context

A workspace with many agents (an import brings twenty or more, for several companies and projects) had one long "Agentes" list in the sidebar. Other agent apps show their agents in titled sections. Nothing here grouped agents, and channels have no sections either, so there was no pattern to follow.

## Decision

1. **Groups belong to the workspace** and everyone in it sees them, like channels. A group has a name (up to 80 characters) and a place among the groups, at most 50 of them. An agent is in one group at most: its group is a column of the agent (`agents.group_id`, with its place inside, `group_position`), so the database holds that rule. Deleting a group sets its agents back to no group (the foreign key does it).
2. **Owners and admins change them** (`manage_agents`, as for the agents themselves). Members read them and close the ones they don't need, on their own screen.
3. **One view for every app.** `GET …/agent-groups` gives the groups in order, each with its agents in order, archived ones left out. Every change (`POST …/agent-groups`, `PATCH` and `DELETE …/agent-groups/{groupId}`, `PUT …/agents/{agentId}/group` with a place inside the group) answers with that whole list and tells the workspace's open apps with the org event `agent.groups`, on which each app reads the list again. The event carries no list: the list grows with the agents (fifty groups of long names, or a couple hundred agents in groups), and Postgres NOTIFY, which carries org events between replicas, refuses payloads near 8000 bytes. Agents in no group keep the workspace's order (when they were created). The refusals a person can meet carry codes the apps translate: `agent_group.too_many` (with `max`) and `agent_group.not_found` (a group someone else deleted meanwhile).
4. **Web and desktop app.** The agents in no group stay under "Agentes", and each group is a category after it, which closes like the others (remembered in the browser). For owners and admins: "+" on "Agentes" makes a group; "…" on a group renames it, moves it up or down, or deletes it after a question; "…" on an agent's row, shown on hover, picks its group, makes a new one with it, or moves it up or down inside its group; and rows drag between categories, to a place inside a group. The agent's page has a Grupo field in Configurações › Identidade, saved with the rest of the page and shown once the workspace has groups. A workspace with no groups sees the sidebar as before. *Amended 2026-10-30:* the sidebar lists only the agents the person can talk to, from the server's `canTalk` (the agent's audience and `talk_to_agents`, ADR 0085; `listsAgent` in `packages/client/src/lib/audience.ts`, so a later limit per person reaches every list unchanged), and a group with none of those doesn't show for them. The same rule holds in their @ suggestions, ⌘K and agent pickers (Criar canal, adding to a channel, a task's assignee). An empty channel's suggestions, its "/" commands and the mission they open go, for everyone, to an agent that answers the person. Who manages agents (`manage_agents`) sees every agent, a locked one with the lock that says who it answers, and every group, empty ones too: they arrange them. Equipe stays the directory, every agent for everyone, and messages agents already posted stay as they are. *Amended 2026-11-02:* the sidebar no longer lists agents (its "Mensagens diretas" part lists conversations, people and agents together). The same arrangement moved to Equipe's "Grupos de agentes" card (`TeamGroups.tsx`): the agents in no group under "Agentes" and every group for who manages agents, who also make, rename, order and delete groups and move agents there by the "…" (always shown, touch screens too) or by dragging; everyone else reads the groups holding an agent they can talk to. *Amended 2026-11-03:* the sidebar's AGENTES part comes back between Canais and Mensagens diretas, fixed for what made the old one confusing (§7).
5. **Phone.** "Esperando você" and "Trabalhando agora" stay first. "Livres" splits into the agents in no group ("Sem grupo" once there are groups) and one section per group, which collapses and is remembered on the phone, on one list for every workspace (group ids are unique). Agents move between groups on the web for now. *Amended 2026-10-30:* for someone who doesn't manage agents, Agentes, the home's greeting (who works, who rests) and its Agentes (the DMs with agents, with the tab's badge) and adding to a channel leave out the agents that don't answer them, by the same rule as the web. The home waits for the agents before it shows, so those DMs don't flash in. Someone whom no agent answers reads that on Agentes, not "no agents yet". *Amended 2026-11-03:* the pins of §7 hold on the phone too: Conversas' Agentes, Canais and Pessoas show their pinned rows first, with a small pin, and a long press on any row has Fixar no topo (and a channel's Mover para…); Agentes' Livres starts with the pinned agents that are free.
6. **Imports.** `createAgentGroup(deps, userId, orgId, { name, agentIds })` makes a group after the others with these agents in it, in order, taking them out of any other group. An importer that brings sections of agents calls it once per section.

7. **The sidebar's AGENTES, again (2026-11-03).** The beta's paying customer wanted the groups back in the sidebar; the old part read as "agents I can't use" for admins and listed agent DMs twice. Now:
   - **Who shows:** the agents that talk to me (`canTalk`), the right hand apart on top. Who manages agents may turn on "Mostrar agentes que não falam comigo" in the part's ⋯; such a row says who it talks to under the name ("só fala com Suporte") with the lock. A member whom no agent answers gets no part; with no agents at all, who creates agents gets the part with its + (Criar agente) and no row of its own.
   - **Rows:** the agents in no group first with no header, then a header per group with its count. A group starts closed past twelve agents; closed, it still shows the agents that work, wait for a computer or wrote to me (`data-live`), and how many work. While an agent works, the line under its name says where ("trabalhando em #produto") in place of the old pill; out of office, the row rests dim with its "volta às" line. "Ver todos os N agentes" ends the part, to Equipe. An empty group shows only to managers, with "Arraste agentes para cá".
   - **Mensagens diretas** lists people only: an agent's row in AGENTES is its DM. With no conversation with a person yet it says so, with Nova mensagem.
   - **Order, per person:** do workspace, Meus primeiro (the agents I'm responsible for lead each group) or Trabalhando primeiro.
   - **Arranging (managers):** drag a row to another group or place, or the row's right click "Mudar de grupo" (the keyboard's way) and "Novo grupo com …"; either says so in a toast with Desfazer. The part's + has Criar agente and Novo grupo; renaming, ordering and deleting groups stay on Equipe ("Organizar grupos na Equipe").
   - **Fixar no topo:** any row's right click (an agent, a channel I'm in, a person), the cards' ⋯ and the channel header's menu pin it on top of its own section (AGENTES, CANAIS, MENSAGENS DIRETAS) above a thin line, five per section; the sixth says to unpin one. There is no Favoritos section, so kinds never mix. Until the person first pins or unpins, their own agents in no group (up to five, the most recently active first) are pinned (`pins: null` → `dayOnePins`). A grouped agent stays in its group: a pin takes an agent out of it, and these pins follow the DMs' activity, so grouped agents pinned this way jumped out of their groups whenever agents talked.
   - **Equipe** is one of the tools on top (`team` in `SIDEBAR_ITEMS`, shown by default), no longer an icon on a header.
   - **Saved per person and workspace, on every device:** `GET`/`PUT …/me/sidebar/view` (`{ agentSort, showAllAgents, pins }`), kept as `view` in `organization_members.sidebar_items` beside the tools' places and both orders, so no migration. A stored view is cleaned on read (`sidebarViewOf`: known orders, each pin once, five at most); a pin of something gone or no longer visible simply doesn't show.

## Consequences

- Changes to groups are not in the audit log, like the board's columns: they change how lists read, not what an agent may do.
- An archived agent keeps its group and is out of every view while archived.
- Touch screens show no "…" on a row (there is no hover): there, the agent's page moves an agent.
- Two moves at the same time may give two agents the same place in a group. The order then falls back on when they were created, and the next move numbers the group again.
- Pins are ids the server doesn't check against channels, agents or people: the apps show only the ones the person still sees, and a stale one costs one of the five places until it is unpinned. Checking them would mean reading three tables on every save for no visible gain.
- "Trabalhando primeiro" reorders as agents start and stop; a drop while it is on lands by what the person sees, which can differ from the workspace's order inside the group.
- An import run twice makes its groups twice unless the importer looks for a group of the same name first (`listAgentGroups`).

## Amendment (2026-11-04): groups are setores (ADR 0148)

The groups are shown as setores ("departments" in English): the same rows, the same moves and the same permissions. Each one gains a color and an icon (the roles' sets, never sky), a lead and a substitute (`agent_groups.color`, `icon`, `lead_user_id`, `backup_user_id`, migration 0219, set with `PATCH …/agent-groups/{groupId}`). Equipe's "Grupos de agentes" card became Equipe › Setores, one card per setor. The sidebar's headers carry the setor's mark.

## Amendment (2026-11-10): a pin keeps an agent in its setor, a setor's menu on its header

### Context

People said the setores didn't stay organized in the sidebar, that pinning an agent didn't work right, and that they couldn't find how to rename or delete a setor. A pin took an agent out of its setor: an agent put in a setor any other way than by the person's own move (by another admin, the agent's page, the right hand) kept its old pin, so it stayed on top and its setor looked empty or short, unlike Equipe. A new pin went to the end of the pinned rows, where the row next to them barely moved. A pin of something gone (a deleted agent, a channel left) still held one of the five places, and nothing showed it to take off. Renaming and deleting were only on Equipe › Setores, and the sidebar's "Organizar setores na Equipe" opens Equipe on its last view.

### Decision

- **A pin never takes an agent out of its setor.** Pinned rows lead their own section: the pinned agents in no setor on top of AGENTES, a pinned agent in a setor on top of that setor (`splitPinned`), on the web and in the phone's Livres. A setor shows every agent in it, whoever put it there and whenever. Putting an agent in a setor by hand still takes off the person's pin, so it lands where it was dropped.
- **Fixar no topo puts the row first** among its section's pins (`togglePin`), on the web as the phone already did.
- **A pin of something gone holds no place.** The apps leave out the pins of agents, channels and people no longer in their lists before counting the five and when they save (`pinsOf` with `known`); a list not loaded yet keeps its pins. This replaces the consequence above that a stale pin costs a place until it is unpinned.
- **A setor's menu on its header.** Who manages agents gets a "…" on each setor's header in the sidebar, and the same choices on the header's right click: Editar (name, color and icon), Mover para cima or para baixo, Excluir setor after a question. It is Equipe › Setores' menu (`useGroupEntries`). On the phone, each setor's header in Agentes has a "…" and a long press with Editar, which opens the setor's page, where who manages agents now renames it, and Excluir setor. The phone's channel category headers show the same "…" for their long press menu. "Organizar setores na Equipe" in AGENTES' "…" opens Equipe on Setores.
