# 0139. Delegated approvals: who approves for me

- Status: Accepted and built, both parts: a person covers (§1) and the right hand decides (§2), in the engine, server, the shared client, the web and the phone app.
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Amends: ADR 0015 (who decides an approval), ADR 0126 (the people a `request_approval` names may be covered), ADR 0081 §4 (the right hand may decide another agent's proposal, within code limits), ADR 0129 (approvals stay a person's, except this bounded delegation), ADR 0137 §4 (the right hand decides for a person, never in its chats) and §5 (`propose_settings_change` is any setup agent's)
- Extends: ADR 0084 §14 (out of office now routes approvals), ADR 0053 (the person behind a run, stored on the approval), ADR 0085 §7 (nobody gives what they can't do)

## Context

A customer asked whether a team manager could approve everything for them. A role with `approve_actions` already makes someone approve every agent action in the workspace, but there was no "only mine", no "while I'm away" and no "on behalf of" in the record. And the agent version (the right hand approves for me) went against ADR 0081, 0129 and 0137 §4 as written.

## Decision

### 1. A person covers ("Quem aprova por mim")

- One cover per person in a workspace (`approval_covers`, unique per `org_id` and `from_user_id`): one other member, `always` or `while_out` (only while the person's status says out of office, ADR 0084 §14, read live with its end), until an optional `ends_at`, and `risky` (merge, deploy and other high-risk actions), off by default. Both people must be members: the row goes with either one (composite foreign keys, cascade).
- **What it reaches:** only what is asked of the person personally: as the agent's owner, or named by `request_approval` (a person named, or a role they hold, resolved when it was asked). What someone decides only through a workspace-wide key (`approve_actions`) isn't theirs to pass on: other admins already decide it, and a workspace that wants a human approver for everything gives a role.
- **Never, whatever the settings (code):** the cover's own request (`approvals.requested_by_user_id`, the person behind the run as the Tool Gateway saw it, and `approvers.requestedBy`), an action in someone's own mailbox (`approvers.required`, only its owner decides), access for good (`request_permission`, admins), approving for good ("Aprovar sempre", 409 `approval.always_unavailable`: a standing rule outlives the cover and changes the agent for everyone). High-risk actions only with `risky`: `approvals.high_risk`, set when asked from the tool's name and scope (`highRisk`: what "Aprovar sempre" never covers by name, plus deploy, rollback, delete, send and the like, mirroring the client's `approvalRisk`); rows from before count as high risk. Money, roles, keys, invitations, archiving and deleting aren't approvals at all and stay with the person.
- **No re-delegation:** a cover reaches only what is asked of the covered person, never what reaches them as someone's cover, so A→B never passes to C.
- **The delegate never decides more than the delegator could:** the cover's reach is computed from what the covered person is asked now (owner, named, still a member), live at each decision, not from a snapshot.
- **Deciding:** the same `decideApproval` and "Aprovar tudo" as any decider; the row keeps `decided_by_user_id` (the cover) and `decided_for_user_id` (the person), the audit row `on_behalf_of_user_id`, and the run reads "Approved by Bruno (covering Ana)". Someone who may decide by their own right (an admin, the owner) never decides "covering".
- **Routing (`approverIds`):** whoever covers someone asked personally is asked too: card badge, inbox, push and the reminder email. While the person is out, only the cover is asked; the person still decides from Aprovações (`decidableBySql`), without a push. Setting, changing or ending a cover, and going out or coming back, move what waits (`rerouteApprovals`): it reaches whoever is asked now and leaves whoever isn't.
- **Hearing it:** the cover gets an inbox entry when chosen (`approval_cover`, inbox only); the person hears when the cover says "Não quero cobrir" or an admin ends it. Ending a cover: the person, the cover, or who manages members (`DELETE …/approval-covers/{userId}`).
- **Screens:** Configurações › Você › Aprovações on the web, Perfil › Aprovações on the phone: Pessoa, Quando, Até, the risky switch, the line of what always stays with you, and Você aprova por with Não quero cobrir. A decided card reads "Aprovado por Bruno, cobrindo Ana" ("cobrindo você" for Ana).

### 2. The right hand decides ("O Gerente aprova por você")

- **Who:** only the workspace's right hand (ADR 0137), under the name the workspace gave it ("Aprovado por Gerente em nome de Ana"; the copy has no article, so any name reads right). A custom agent never decides: it reads the web, apps and mail all day.
- **What, in code (two kinds):** `agent_new`, an `agent_proposal` card another agent posted, only with no apps, mailboxes or routine and only open channels (public, not a DM), created as the person (`create_agents`, live) with the workspace's key (never a plan of theirs) and a budget of the smaller of the workspace's default and the person's cap; `agent_settings`, a settings card of one agent (not the right hand, not archived) whose every line is the model on the same AI account (same provider, not a computer brain), a budget, or joining, leaving or the reply mode in an open channel, each row one the person may decide now (`maySettle`), and what it adds to that agent's budget this month within the cap. Anything else on the card (owner, who talks, AI account, archive) leaves it to the person. `propose_settings_change` is now any setup agent's tool (it was the right hand's only), so another agent's settings card exists to decide.
- **Only the person's own request:** the run that posted the card is not a routine's, its chain roots in a message the person wrote (`messageRoot`), and the card is for them (`forUserId`, `requested_by_user_id`). Another member's bot, an agent's own work, e-mail, webhooks and routines go to people as before. Someone else writing into a run of the chain while it works (another person, or an agent whose message isn't rooted in the same person: `meta.steeredRunIds`) sends the card to the person too: their words reached it outside the request. A settings card on an agent of the chain itself (an agent raising its own budget) is always the person's.
- **Never its own, no loops, no generations:** a card the right hand posted, or one whose chain passed through any run of it (`runChain`: an agent asked it, it asked an agent), reads "não decide o próprio pedido". A card from an agent one of its decisions created goes to the person. The decision is an outbox job (`right_hand.decide`, once per card), never a chat turn: nobody can mention or DM it into deciding, and its chats have no decide tool.
- **The call:** one Model Gateway call as the right hand (its budget checked first, usage recorded with its `agent_id` and the `org_id`), the model the workspace's Automático picks on the medium tier, 60 s. The prompt holds the person's own words (their request and guidance), the team, the limits code already checked, and the card as data in a fence it can't close (`<` and `>` escaped). It may answer approve or pass, never refuse; anything but a clean `{"decision":"approve"}` is a pass (`readDecision`), as are no AI, a spent budget, an error or the timeout. The model only narrows: what it may approve was decided before it was called.
- **Caps (code):** 10 decision calls and 2 new agents a day (24 hours), US$ 50 a month added to budgets through it; the person sets only the budget per agent (default US$ 20, at most US$ 50). A cap reached reads "chegou ao limite de hoje". The row that holds a card's place under the caps, and the budget per agent, are checked and written under one lock per person before the call (`deciding`), so two cards at once can't both fit.
- **Applied as the person:** `createProposedAgent` and `decideProposal` as them, the right hand's name in what the proposing agent reads ("Gerente, on behalf of Ana, created it"); a person deciding first wins. The audit row `right_hand.approved` has the right hand as actor and `on_behalf_of_user_id`.
- **The card:** `meta.actionCard.delegate` (`card.delegate` event): "está analisando em nome de Ana…", then "Triagem · Aprovado por Gerente em nome de Ana · Desfazer · Por quê?" (its reason, and what Desfazer does until when), or for the person alone "passou para você: …", "Fora do que você deixou Gerente aprovar", "não decide o próprio pedido".
- **Desfazer, 24 hours:** by the person or who manages agents; a new agent is archived (the person may archive what was made for them), settings go back through their receipts. Three undone in a week pause it (`paused_at`, a waiting `right_hand_paused` that pushes) until the person turns it back on.
- **Hearing it:** what it approves pushes nothing: one quiet inbox entry a day (`right_hand_decided`, merging, `count`). What it passes stays the card it was, which reaches the person as before.
- **Settings:** Configurações › Você › Aprovações and Perfil › Aprovações: one switch, the two kinds (greyed "Você não decide isso hoje" when the person can't), the budget per agent and optional guidance (2,000 characters). What stays with the person closes the page.

## Consequences

- One more reason someone can decide an approval: Aprovações, the card and the audit row say "cobrindo".
- An approval asked while its person was in reaches the cover the moment they go out, and comes back when they return. An out-of-office status that ends by its own time (no write) moves nothing until the next change, though deciding follows it at once.
- Skipped until someone asks: several covers in order, per-agent routing, an admin's view of a member's cover on the member's page (an admin may already end it through the API).
- Residual risk, stated: a run the person started can still read attacker text mid-run and propose. The worst outcome is an agent with no apps, in open channels, on at most the person's cap, that they archive with one tap; settings stay on the same AI account and within the cap.
- Skipped from the design until asked: the same requester's identical card passed once a day goes straight to the person (the day's caps bound repeats), admins' view of a member's right-hand setting, the e-mail digest.
