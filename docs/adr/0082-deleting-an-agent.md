# 0082. Deleting an agent: a tombstone that names what it left

- Status: Accepted (built: the server, the shared client, the web and desktop agent page, the phone)
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Amends: ADR 0056 (§5: the `agent.deleted` event)
- Code: `packages/engine/src/agent-deletion.ts` (`deleteAgent`), `packages/engine/src/agents.ts` (`getAgent`, `listFormerAgents`, `deletedHandle`), `packages/engine/src/machine-accounts.ts` (`releaseAgentLogin`), `packages/engine/src/secrets.ts` (`removeAgentSecrets`), `packages/engine/src/routines.ts` (`deleteAgentRoutines`), `packages/engine/src/cancel.ts` (`stopOrgWork` for one agent), `packages/engine/src/run-inbox.ts` (`handOn`), `apps/server/src/routes/app/agents.ts`, `packages/client/src/lib/directory.ts`, `apps/web/src/pages/AgentPage.tsx`, `apps/mobile/src/features/agents/DeleteAgentScreen.tsx`

## Context

An agent could only be archived: it left the lists and its routines stopped, while its instructions, memory, grants, budget, login and channel memberships stayed. Nothing gave a reason to keep agents forever, and people want to remove one for good, an imported duplicate for example. An agent's messages, runs and usage point at its row, and the workspace's reports and old conversations need its name.

## Decision

1. **Who.** Owners and admins: `manage_agents`, the permission that creates and archives (members don't have it). Archived agents can be deleted too. Deleting is permanent: there is no restore.
2. **The tombstone.** The row stays with `deleted_at` (and `archived_at`, if it wasn't archived), keeping only its display fields: name, role and look. Every other setting goes back to its default: its instructions and model are emptied, and its key, owner, budget, audience, computer, brain and login, browser settings, missions' default and any operator hold cleared. Its handle becomes `deleted-<id>`, longer than the 32 characters a handle may have, so nobody can take it and no mention matches it, and the old handle is free at once. `getAgent` reads a deleted agent as missing, so every path that loads an agent (runs, tools, routes, the agent page) treats it as gone, and an update or a second delete answers 404.
3. **What goes, at once.** First it is archived, so nothing new starts (every list, picker, mention and router reads only agents that aren't archived). Its open missions close as canceled (`concludeMission`), its routines are deleted as a person deletes one (their run history stays), and its runs, computer tasks and desktop stop (`stopOrgWork` for that agent: their approvals, questions and secret requests close, and what was sent to its runs is dropped, since no run ending hands anything on to an archived agent, `handOn`), and the secrets people delivered to it leave its computers (§4). Then, in one transaction: its memory and notes (with their search entries), instruction proposals and outcomes, skill bindings, scopes and repositories, mailbox and database grants, budget periods and reservations, and channel memberships are deleted; its pending approvals and open handoffs close; its DMs (and its routine rooms) are archived; and its login becomes a tombstone (§4). The approvals, questions and handoffs it left waiting in people's inboxes are resolved.
4. **Its computer.** Its login, the account that thinks only for it (ADR 0059 §2), becomes a tombstone as when a person removes an account, and the runner signs it out and deletes its folder now, or at its next hello. The account's other logins stay with their agents, and so does one made for all of its owner's agents (ADR 0059 §2, amended 2026-10-30): it thinks for no agent alone. One agent per login counts only agents that aren't archived, so an archived agent's login may have been given to a live agent since: that login stays with the live agent, and only the tombstone lets go of it. The secrets people delivered to it (ADR 0027) leave the computer they were delivered to, before the tombstone, through the runner's own removal, as removing one from the agent's page does (`removeAgentSecrets`; their rows stay as the record, marked removed). On a computer that doesn't answer then (offline), a secret stays in the runner's secrets folder until the computer is wiped, and its row stays open. The computer's agent list drops it, so the runner stops its desktop and, in system mode, takes back the account folders it lent its user. The runner has no removal path for an agent's own folder: in personal mode `<handle>-<id8>` stays in the workspace folder, and in system mode its user `ag-<id8>` and its home stay until the computer is wiped (ADR 0057: users are never removed, so their uids are never reused). Nothing runs as them again, and a new agent never gets them, since all of them carry the id.
5. **What stays.** Its messages and threads, runs, usage records (with their costs) and audit rows. Usage keeps pointing at the tombstone: the workspace's totals keep its cost and the reports that name agents by row (plan usage by account and day) show the name it had, while the per-agent budget list shows the team only, as for an archived agent.
6. **Names in the apps.** `GET …/former-agents` lists the agents no longer in the team (archived or deleted) with name, role, look and whether it was deleted. The directories of the web, desktop and phone keep them apart from the team (`anyAgent`): message authors, faces and reactions read them (on the web and desktop the cards in their messages and inbox entries too), no list, picker or mention offers them, and their profile card says only that they are no longer in the workspace.
7. **The API and the screens.** `DELETE …/agents/{agentId}?purge=true` (without it the route still archives). On the web and the desktop app it is the second row of Configurações › Zona de perigo; on the phone, a row of "Editar agente" opening a screen of its own. An archived agent has no page, so admins find it under Arquivados, at the end of Equipe (web and desktop) and of Agentes (phone), where only the delete is offered; `former-agents` says which former agents are deleted (`deleted`). Both screens say what goes and what stays and enable the delete only once the agent's name is typed (`matchesName`: case, extra spaces, width forms and invisible characters aside, as for a workspace, ADR 0088 §1).
8. **Recorded.** `agent.deleted` in the audit log (with the name and handle it had) and, with the operator API on, on the operator feed (`data: { agentId }`).

## Consequences

- An archived agent's old messages now show its name and face too, where they said "unknown agent".
- A deleted agent's old messages may mention its old handle, which can belong to a new agent by then: the text then reads as that agent, while the mention itself (stored by id) still points at the old one.
- Run rows and their transcripts stay with its messages; they are only reachable through the messages and audit rows they back.

## Alternatives considered

- **Deleting the row.** Its foreign keys would take its runs, usage records and approvals with it, and the messages would lose their author. Rejected.
- **Keeping the handle on the tombstone behind a partial unique index.** Every handle lookup and the import's dedupe would have to skip deleted rows; the renamed handle needs no reader to change. Rejected.
- **A runner operation that deletes the agent's folder and user.** It would need a protocol version, an offline queue and a careful `userdel` in system mode; left for when disk space on shared computers asks for it.
