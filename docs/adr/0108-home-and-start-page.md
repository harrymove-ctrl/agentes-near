# 0108. Início, the workspace's home, and what opens when the app starts

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-31
- Code: `packages/db/src/schema/tenancy.ts` (`organization_members.start_page`, `start_channel_id`), `packages/engine/src/organizations.ts` (`setStartPage`, `listMemberships`), `apps/server/src/routes/app/account.ts` (`GET /me`), `apps/server/src/routes/app/management.ts` (`PUT …/me/start`); shared: `packages/client/src/lib/home.ts` (`startPlace`, the home's sections, the first steps), `working.ts` (`workingNow`), `notifications.ts` (`waitingCardOf`), `format.ts` (`upcomingAt`), `api/queries.ts` (`useSetStart`, `useAskAgent`); web: `apps/web/src/routes/org.$orgId.index.tsx`, `org.$orgId.home.tsx`, `apps/web/src/pages/HomePage.tsx`, `apps/web/src/lib/start.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/Sidebar.tsx`; phone: `apps/mobile/src/features/me/logic.ts` (`startHref`), `features/me/useStart.ts`, `StartPage` in `features/me/PreferencePages.tsx`, `features/chats/ChatsScreen.tsx`, `features/chats/FirstSteps.tsx`, `features/chat/ChannelHello.tsx`

## Context

Opening the web or desktop app went to #general. In a new workspace that is an empty channel ("Diga oi em #general" and three suggestions for its agent, which grew wider than their dashed card on a phone: a button never wraps its text). In a busy one it says nothing about what waits on you, what your agents are doing or what they finished. A customer asked to choose which screen opens, or for a starting page with a summary.

Everything such a page needs is already loaded somewhere: the inbox (approvals, agents' questions, mentions, what finished: `packages/engine/src/notifications.ts`), missions waiting on you (ADR 0029), the agents at work and their lines (`active-runs`, ADR 0084), routines and their next run (ADR 0053), and the onboarding's own signs of a new workspace. The phone's home tab, Conversas, already opens on "Esperando você" and a greeting that says what an agent finished and who works.

## Decision

1. **Início, a page of its own** (`/org/{orgId}/home`, first in the sidebar). One column that answers, top to bottom, "what needs me, what are my agents doing, what got done":
   - **The greeting** ("Boa tarde, Heitor") and one line: how many things wait on you and how many agents work, or "Tudo em dia".
   - **Peça a um agente**: a composer with the agent in a picker (the agents that answer me, the one I talked to last first). Sending opens that DM (made if new) with the message already sent, the agent at work in front of me.
   - **Esperando você**: the inbox's waiting items (approvals, agents' questions, help in a browser, requests to join), missions with a decision, review or command for me, and unread mentions. Each row says who and what, then the exact request, where and when, and has one button (Revisar, Responder, Abrir) that opens it where the inbox would.
   - **Trabalhando agora**: each agent at work (runs in the conversations I'm in, active missions), with its line (ADR 0084) or else what it works on, where and since when. It opens the conversation.
   - **Desde a sua última visita**: what agents finished that I haven't seen (unread `mission_finished`, `routine_finished`, `agent_reply`, and a task moved to done), from the last 7 days. Unread is the inbox's own "you haven't seen it", read on any device, so no visit time is kept for it.
   - **Próximas rotinas**: the next three runs of active scheduled routines, with the agent and when ("hoje 18:00", "amanhã 09:00").
   A section shows only with something in it, at most five rows, then "Ver tudo" to its page (Notificações, Rotinas).
2. Amended 2026-11-07 (ADR 0155 §6): the first steps below are gone. While the right hand's first-run checklist has a row left, Início (and Conversas on the phone) shows one line, "Continue de onde parou · 2 de 5", that opens its DM, so two checklists can never disagree, and the greeting's "Comece por aqui" follows it. The rest of this item is history.
   **A new workspace starts with "Primeiros passos"**: create an agent, connect an AI, ask an agent something, invite the team, create a routine. Each is checked from what the apps already load (the agents, the AI status, the DMs, the people, the routines), shown only to who may do it, with one button that does it. Connecting an AI is for who may add the workspace's key or owns an agent (a plan thinks only for its owner's agents, ADR 0059): anyone else would open a dialog that sends them to someone else. The card leaves once every step shown is done, or when hidden (this browser). The line under the greeting says "Comece por aqui" only while an agent, its AI or a first request is still to do: inviting and a routine can stay open for good (a workspace of one), and a workspace in use isn't new. A set-up workspace with nothing pending shows one calm line instead of empty sections. On the phone the same steps (`firstSteps`, `stepPermissions`) are a section of Conversas, above the conversations, closed and opened like the others, each a row that opens its screen. The phone has no hide for them, so the section shows only while the workspace is starting out and leaves once an agent, its AI and a first request are done.
3. **No new storage or endpoint for the page.** It reads the lists Notificações, Aprovações, Threads and Rotinas read, under their own query keys (opening those pages after Início is instant, and their live updates reach it), plus `agent-status`, which the sidebar already polls every 30 s, and `active-runs`, the only new query on the web (the phone already reads it). Início follows no channel, so their `agent.run` and `run.finished` never reach it: it asks `active-runs` again every 20 s while it shows (`WORKING_POLL_MS`, as Conversas does on the phone).
4. **What opens when the app starts** is the person's, per workspace, kept on the server so the web, the desktop app and the phone follow one choice: `organization_members.start_page` is `home` (the default), `last` or `channel` (`start_channel_id`). `GET /me` sends it with each workspace (`start`), so opening decides with no request of its own, and `PUT /organizations/{orgId}/me/start` sets it (a channel must be one of this workspace's, not a DM nor archived, with the person in it: `start.channel_unknown`).
   - `home`: the app's home. Início on the web, Conversas on the phone.
   - `last`: where the person was. The web keeps the last page shown in each workspace, the phone the last conversation opened, each on its device (a place is a device's).
   - `channel`: that channel while the person is in it and it isn't archived, else the home.
   - **When it applies.** On the web, when a workspace opens: the app's address, the workspace's bare address (`/org/{orgId}`, from the switcher, an email, a reload) or another workspace chosen. Never on a move inside the workspace: leaving a channel goes to Início, and so does a bare address that carries a request (`?connect=ai`). On the phone, when the app starts on Conversas: the chosen conversation is pushed over it (back is Conversas), never over a screen a notification or a link opened.
   - **Where it is chosen.** On Início's header ("Ao abrir o app"), on the phone in Você › Ao abrir o app. A phone on an older self-hosted server, which sends no choice and can't keep one, shows no such row and opens Conversas.
5. **An empty conversation's suggestions stay inside their card at every width.** An empty state's actions wrap, and a button in them wraps its text and is never wider than the card. At a phone's width the channel's suggestions stack, each as wide as the card. The phone's empty channel says the same, "Diga oi em #canal", with the same three suggestions as rows in a card (a tap sends, as the first conversation with an agent does), in place of a quiet line.

## Consequences

- #general is no longer where the web opens. The onboarding still ends there (its first message is in it), and every other bare workspace address opens Início unless the person chose otherwise.
- "Desde a sua última visita" follows reading, not time: something seen on the phone isn't news on the web, and something never opened stays until read, for 7 days.
- `last` on a new device starts at the home, and a page that is gone (a channel left or archived) falls back to it.
- A shared device opens each account at its own choice: the places are kept per workspace, and sign-out clears the phone's.
- Open, for the owner to decide: Conversas carries only Início's first steps. Its greeting already says what an agent finished and who works, its "Esperando você" holds what waits (a mention shows as an unread conversation), and Atividade shows the next routine. Bringing over "Desde a sua última visita", the unread mentions, "Próximas rotinas" or an ask-an-agent composer is left out until then. The choice could also offer a DM or a thread.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the choice on each device.** Simpler, but the person would choose twice, and "per person" would not hold on a new phone.
- **One new endpoint for the whole page.** Fewer requests on the first visit, but a second copy of what the inbox, missions and routines already answer, with its own events to keep live.
- **A dashboard of counts and charts.** It says how much, not what to do next. Rows that each open the exact thing are what a person acts on.
