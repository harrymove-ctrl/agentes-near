# 0010. Real-time over native Bun WebSocket and a `PubSub` adapter

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-14

## Decision

- **Endpoint** `GET /api/ws?orgId=<uuid>`, served by the same `Bun.serve` as the HTTP app (`apps/server/src/realtime/ws.ts`). The upgrade requires a Better Auth session cookie, an allowed `Origin` (prevents cross-site WebSocket hijacking), and membership of `orgId`.
- **A socket lives as long as its session.** The upgrade reads the session from the database, never Better Auth's signed cookie cache, and the socket keeps its session id. A session Better Auth deletes (sign-out, "sign out everywhere", a password reset, a deleted user) is announced on the server-side topic `session:<userId>`, and every socket opened with it closes on every replica: `/api/ws` with `{"type":"error","code":"session_ended"}` and code 4401, a desktop or terminal stream (ADR 0058) with 4403 `signed_out`. Endings nobody announces (an expired session, rows deleted outside Better Auth) are caught by a lookup of every open socket's session each 60 s (`realtime/session-watch.ts`), and by the streams' own 30 s recheck.
- **Subscriptions:** the client sends `{"type":"subscribe","channelId":…}`. The server allows it only if `requireChannelAccess` passes for that org (same check as HTTP); otherwise it answers `{"type":"error","code":"forbidden"|"not_found"}`. At most 200 subscriptions per socket, 60 frames per 10 s, 16 KB frames.
- **Events** (`ChannelEvent` in `@agenteschat/engine`), forwarded as `{"type":"event","channelId","event"}`:
  - `message.created` — committed message (inline when small; otherwise `messageId` with its `threadId` and `agentRunId`, fetch it over HTTP: the app ends that run's live answer and holds its text until the read lands);
  - `agent.run` — `running` (typing indicator), `done`, `failed` with a reason;
  - `agent.typing` — the agent writes (a model call's first text). What it writes never goes out while it works (amended 2026-11-10): its reply shows whole, once, when `message.created` posts it, at the end of the conversation, so nothing on screen appears and then changes. The apps say who works, on what and for how long in the one line above the composer (`agent.thinking`, `agent.tool` with its step's words, `agent.typing`), and a reply takes no place before what people sent while it worked. Apps still read an `agent.delta` from an older server as "it writes", never its text.
  - `message.reactions` — someone reacted (emoji, or `+1`/`-1`) or took it back: the message's full reaction list, which replaces the client's.
  - `machine.task` — a background task on a connected computer started, reported progress (last line, files, seconds), ended, was interrupted or resumed: `{ channelId, threadId, task }` with the task's whole view (replace, don't merge); `threadId` null = the main timeline (ADR 0029).
  - `mission.updated` — a mission changed (ADR 0029): `{ channelId, threadId, missionId, status, change }` where `change` is `proposed | started | paused | resumed | stopped | closed | plan | participants | owner | machine | decision | review | suggestion | task | file_edited`, with small details when at hand (`decision`, `review`, `messageId` + `suggestion`, `taskId`, `planVersion`, `canceled`, `outcome`, `code`). Sent to the mission's home channel; clients refetch `GET …/missions/{missionId}` for the rest. Its tasks keep sending `machine.task`. The owner also gets user events `mission.needs_you` (a decision or review), `mission.approval_requested` and `mission.approval_decided`.
  - `thread.updated` — a thread was created from a range, renamed, its range changed, moved out of the channel or back, closed, reopened, archived, unarchived or undone (`change`), with its `state` and, when the server has it, the fresh `thread` view; `messageIds` lists the messages that left or came back to the channel (up to 50; otherwise refetch). Sent to the thread's channel and to the range's channel when different (ADR 0029).
- **Fan-out** goes through the `PubSub` adapter, topic `channel:<id>`. Core: Postgres LISTEN/NOTIFY on one channel (payload limit enforced below 8 KB). Cloud can register Redis. Postgres is the source of truth; real-time is best effort and clients catch up over HTTP on reconnect, and each time the server confirms a channel's subscription (`subscribed`), the first one included: a channel's events reach a socket only from then on, so what was published between a page's read and that moment (an answer that needs no model, posted while the socket opened after a page change) would be missing until a reload. That read adds what the lists lack and takes nothing away: replacing a list would drop a message heard live while the read was on its way (`catchUpChannel` in `@agenteschat/client`, the web app and the phone app since 2026-10-26). A read can be older than events heard while it was on its way, so on the web a receipt the server confirmed takes from it only how the run it follows ended, never an older run's end over a newer one ("Responder agora" starts a new run), unless nothing changed that receipt since the read was asked for.
- **Back to the tab.** The web app reads its open conversation and active runs again when the tab comes back after 10 s or more away (amended 2026-11-03): a socket that died while away takes seconds to be called dead, and one that stayed up may still have missed events.
- **Freshness rule** (amended 2026-11-08). A cache is only as current as the events that reach it, so every way events stop reaching it makes it stale, and its next showing reads it again, whatever its `staleTime`:
  - the last screen of a conversation closes (`openConversation`, web and phone; a thread beside its open channel doesn't count as closing it, and a read still on its way is dropped), or a `channel.activity` comes for a conversation no screen has open; a thread's replies and its header read on every opening;
  - the socket reconnects (web: every query of the workspace; phone: every query), and on the phone a socket opened again after the background counts as a reconnect;
  - a catch-up read that fails hands its lists to the query client, which reads them again with its retries;
  - a read (`GET`) waits at most `READ_TIMEOUT_MS` (30 s): one stuck on a dead connection fails and is tried again, so a screen joining it gets fresh data and the loading bar ends;
  - the server: a replica whose LISTEN came back after a gap (postgres.js reconnecting it, or the replica's own 30 s heartbeat finding it deaf and listening again) closes its app sockets with 1012, and a socket whose event Bun dropped past its backpressure limit closes with 1013; either way the app reconnects and reads what it missed.
- **Presence** is tracked per connection through the `Presence` adapter (in-memory in core).

## Consequences

- One process serves HTTP, WebSocket and the SPA: no separate real-time service.
- Follow-ups: revoke live subscriptions when someone leaves a channel or organization (today the check runs at subscribe time; a reconnect re-checks), presence events to clients, typing indicators for people, `NOTIFY` throughput limits at Cloud scale (Redis adapter).
