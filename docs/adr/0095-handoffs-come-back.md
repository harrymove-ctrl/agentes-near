# 0095. Hand-offs between agents come back

- Status: Accepted (built: the server, the web and the phone app). The release waits on `bun run eval:mentions` and the hand-off cases of `bun run eval:behavior` in six languages
- Date: 2026-10-31
- Supersedes: ADR 0048 (`tell_agent` as a note sent without waiting)
- Amends: ADR 0034 (notes are tracked like questions, nothing expires while a colleague works, what an agent hands on rolls up into its answer), ADR 0053 §10 (what came of a note resumes the work for the person behind it), ADR 0089 §5 (the 15-minute rule) and §6 (where a wake-up held inside an agents' DM shows its card)
- Code: `packages/engine/src/consultations.ts` (`tell_agent`, `closeConsultation`, `handBack`, `settleOwed`, `reportHandOffs`, `onConsultedRunFinished`, `expireConsultations`, `handOffsFrom`), `notices.ts`, `run-resume.ts` (`deliverNoticeToRun`'s `wake`), `run-inbox.ts` (`handOn`), `context.ts` ("# Work you handed off here", the prompt lines), `team.ts`, `follow-through.ts` (`wakesLater`, `FOLLOW_THROUGH_NUDGE_DM`), `executor.ts`, `stretch.ts` (`holdOf`, `peopleRun`, `continueStretch`), migration `0145_agent_handoffs`, `packages/client/src/lib/consult-group.ts`, `packages/client/src/realtime/consults.ts`, the web and phone `ConsultGroup`, `packages/engine/eval/*`

## Context

A customer's case (anonymized as in the evals): a person asked the coordinating agent, Chefe, to have Rita pass her folder and everything she knew to Diogo, because Rita was going to be deleted. Chefe handed it to Rita with `tell_agent` and sent Diogo a heads-up. Rita made a hand-off document and a zip and passed them on. While she worked, the person heard twice that nothing had happened:

- A note was sent without waiting and left no row: nothing said who had the work, and asked "então, como correu?", Chefe had nothing to read.
- Its reply came back only when a judge read it as mattering, as an `agent_note` that woke a run working for nobody, which could not hand anything on. A note that joined a run already at work lost its reply.
- Rita's own hand-off to Diogo ended in Rita's DM, where nobody read Diogo's confirmation.
- Questions expired after 15 minutes while their colleague was still working: the asker heard it was late, and its run was stopped.

## Decision

A hand-off is a tracked row. It comes back by itself, once, with what came of it, and until then the agent and the person both see who has it. No words decide anything: states come from run statuses, and the only judge calls are the existing `noteReplyMatters` and `followThroughVerdict`.

1. **Every note is a row.** Each `tell_agent` note is a row in `agent_consultations` with `kind` `note` (migration 0145: the `agent_consultation_kind` enum, `kind`, `reported_at`, and indexes on `target_run_id` and `(org_id, asker_agent_id, created_at)`; old rows are questions). The note, its row and `startOrJoinRun` go in one transaction, so a note that joins a run at work is known too. Only what a run read is its to answer: a question or note still unread in its inbox when it ends or pauses on a decision goes with its row to the run its inbox is handed to (`handOn`), and waits on no run until one takes it. It keeps the checks of ADR 0034 and 0048 (reachable agent, no note back into the conversation it answers, no cycles, `refusedAtLimit`, the same note once an hour unless the first couldn't be done or was never worked on) and adds `sameRequestRun`: no relaying what the person asked that agent directly. Notes record `chain`; `MAX_CONSULTATION_CHAIN` still applies to `ask_agent` and counts note links, and notes have no depth cap of their own (the stretch bounds them).
2. **What came of it comes back as the answer kind.** A note's result is a `consult_reply` notice (`refs.kind` `note`): the run it wakes works for the person behind the sender's run (authority follows `refs.runId`, ADR 0053), must answer, and may ask or hand work on again. A reply the judge reads as nothing the sender needs (an acknowledgement, a thanks, "nothing came yet, I'll take it when it comes") closes as reported, with no notice. With no judge, or when it fails, the reply counts.
3. **Report once** (`reportHandOffs`). The notes one run sent come back together, once none is still being worked on: queued, running, not started (held by the stretch), ended but not closed yet (its end hook still reading the reply), or waiting on what its colleague handed on. A colleague waiting on a person holds up no one: entering the wait releases the others, after the run's unread messages were handed on, and the wake names who is still out (`refs.pending`). Each close runs one claim after it commits (`reported_at` with row locks), so the last closer sees every row closed and the agent wakes exactly once. Oldest first, every notice but the last is only written (`deliverNoticeToRun` with `wake: false`); the last wakes the agent, and a run of its already at work that takes it gets the earlier ones queued ahead of it. Questions keep their own notice each.
4. **Roll-up** (`handBack`, `settleOwed`). An agent in an agents' DM that handed part of its work on and ended keeps what it owes open. Each sub-result joins that answer (`[@diogo, to whom @rita handed part of it]: …`, or "couldn't: …") in the transaction that closes it, with the agent's run locked, so no sub-result is lost to another or to the run's end. One that arrives while that agent still runs is also read by it at its next step, and joins the answer even if the run ends without reading it. Once nothing it handed on is out, what it owes closes with its reply, read from the run, then what came back. An agent that fails closes what it owes as failed at once. Nothing ever starts a run inside an agents' DM just to read a result: an unread one is dropped when the run's inbox is handed on. People read each part as "diogo: …", without the agents' marker, and not a part that couldn't be done (the delegating agent says so).
5. **No expiry while working.** Every minute, a row whose colleague is queued, running, waiting on a person or its computer, or done but waiting on what it handed on gets 15 more minutes before its deadline, its conversation hears it is still out (`agent.consultation`), and that run is never stopped. Only rows nobody started, or whose run is gone, expire as before: with what they gathered, else expired.
6. **Everyone sees it.**
   - The delegating agent reads "# Work you handed off here" every turn, like its open tasks: this agent's questions and notes from this conversation (its thread in a channel) in the last day, open or closed within the hour, newest first, at most 8, each with where its colleague is (starting, working on it, waiting on a person or its computer, waiting on agents it handed some to, not started past the stretch), the start of the answer until it heard it, and no "@" to copy. Not in routines, background runs or agents' DMs.
   - The person sees the work in the block under the reply that handed it ("Passou para 1 colega", "Chamou 3 colegas" when the turn also asked), with "O Atlas passou uma parte adiante e espera o retorno" and "O Atlas não conseguiu terminar". Notes don't take the working line.
   - A note's recipient now answers the person behind it: its approval, question and secret cards land where the person asked (`consultationOrigin`, `consultedFor` find note rows; the walks up a chain go 10 links), and `ask_user` works there.
7. **What agents read.** `tell_agent` says to give the whole job to the agent that owns it, with everything it needs, and not to send heads-ups to agents that only have to wait; that what comes of it reaches the agent by itself with the rest of that turn's; and, from an agents' DM, that its answer goes back once the colleague is done. "How you work" says a closed way means trying another (another tool, the colleague whose role covers it, a copy instead of a move) before saying what a person must do. The agents' DM tells a question from work, a part only another agent can do, and news of something still to come.
8. **Following through between agents.** The follow-through check also reads a run in an agents' DM that still owes an answer, with its own nudge (its last message goes back as the answer, and nothing wakes it there again). A run that handed part on gets none. `wakesLater` counts this agent's open hand-offs from this conversation, from any run, so a later "te aviso" isn't nudged while one is out. In an agents' DM it counts only the run's own: what another run there handed on goes back with that run's answer.
9. **Held wake-ups.** A wake-up `startOrJoinRun` holds inside an agents' DM posts its pause card where the people of that work read (`holdOf` places it with `peopleRun`), and the run Continuar starts takes the held question or note (its row's `target_run_id`, set in `continueStretch`, also when Continuar joins a run at work).

## Consequences

- No new kind of model call: `noteReplyMatters` runs once per note result, and `followThroughVerdict` about once more per agents'-DM run that answers a row. Fewer runs: no checks on a colleague, no unattended runs woken by note replies, no runs in agents' DMs for sub-results, one wake per turn's notes. Every wake still goes through `startOrJoinRun` or `refusedAtLimit`.
- Each run reads the block with one indexed query, and each close adds one claim statement. The block is about 400 tokens at most, only when hand-offs exist.
- A slow colleague delays the single report of a fan-out. Each part shows live in the group, anyone who asks gets the status from the block, and a colleague waiting on a person holds up no one.
- Notes sent before this keep the `agent_note` path (`noteReply`, now only for a note without a row); it can go one release after deploy. Old phone builds read notes as questions ("Perguntou a…", "espera uma resposta").
- Read-only check after deploy: `select kind, status, reported_at is null, count(*) from agent_consultations where created_at > now() - interval '1 day' group by 1, 2, 3`. Open note rows whose target run ended should be close to zero.

## Left out

- Held notes sent "after" another agent's part (`after`): giving the whole job to its owner, the roll-up, the judge and report-once cover a heads-up, and they would add a nullable message, release recursion and "not sent" states that help only when the model picks them.
- A judge call per note to read its intent: every note is tracked, so nothing needs converting.
- A "gave up" choice in the follow-through verdict: the new "How you work" line covers trying another way; add it when an eval shows agents giving up.
- A real-model eval in CI: evals stay manual (`eval/README.md`) and the release waits on them.
- Files that travel with a hand-off and move to the person's conversation when it comes back: the next step, without a migration.
- Continuar from the person's conversation for a colleague at its step limit: its wrap-up comes back as the result, and the delegating agent decides whether to hand it again.

## Addendum (2026-11-01): a mention never reaches nobody

An agent's @mention of an agent of the workspace that isn't a member of the conversation used to be plain text: no run, and only the author read about it in its next context. Now, once an agent's message is dispatched (`message.posted`), `settleReach` (`reach.ts`) settles every agent it named and didn't wake:

- **Outside the conversation**: the mention judge (ADR 0089) reads it with the members; one it needs goes as a note (`sendNote`, the delivery `tell_agent` uses), so its result comes back as any note's does. Only when the person behind the message, or the creator of the routine set to act behind it (ADR 0053 §4), may talk to that agent; a routine that only looks reaches none; an agent already in the chain or already told by this run gets nothing new.
- **Not woken for who is behind it**: a member the requester can't talk to (`dispatchMentions` now returns them as `blocked`).

Each is written on the message (`meta.reach`: agent, reason, the note's consultation, whether it was outside, whose reach fell short, the routine), drawn under it on web and phone (with Ver, Adicionar ao canal for who manages channels, and "Deixar … falar com …" for who may change the agent's audience), and read by the author in its next context. Every skip goes in the message's `routing_decisions` row with one of three reasons: `not_in_channel`, `audience`, `routine_creator_reach`. When a routine set to act couldn't reach an agent, by a mention or by its own `tell_agent`/`ask_agent` (the refusal's `data.reach`, shown under the run's next message, its report included), the agent's owner gets `agent_unreached` (once per routine and agent; who manages agents when it has no owner), opening the agent's page, with the creator in `source.userId` for a one-click "let them talk to it". The routine dialog warns, without blocking, about agents its prompt names that its creator won't reach.

A message is forwarded whole from any conversation but the agents' DM (ADR 0097's default-allow addendum; it used to be only from a public channel, with a `private` line elsewhere, which old messages still show). A target with no AI is never shown as handed (`noAi`). The router decides an agent's message before its reach is recorded, so naming an agent outside the channel doesn't stop a member from chiming in, and a `tell_agent` after a mention already handed sends no second note.

What doesn't change: who may wake whom. The limit of a routine's creator and an agent's audience stay as they were; this only makes hitting them visible.

## Addendum (2026-11-05): an ask that doesn't move ends, and a held one isn't lost

- **The loss.** A note the brakes deferred for room (ADR 0131 §7, `LATER_JOB`) had no run, so the sweep closed it after 15 minutes as "never got to it" while its wake kept looking for up to a day. The sender said the note hadn't arrived and sent it again, and the first one later woke a run whose answer went nowhere. Now a row whose wake still waits (`LATER_JOB`, `INBOX_JOB`) stays open like one with a run, and the tool's result says it waits for room.
- **The limit.** Past `CONSULTATION_HUNG_MS` (2 hours) without moving, a row ends `failed` with why: its colleague never started, or sat in its computer's line, on a computer that's away, on its plan or budget, or on its provider. Its run or its wake goes with it, so no work lands where nobody reads it. The clock is the wait's own (`roomWaitSince`, `waitingForBrain.since`, `outage.since`), not the ask's: hours of work before one refused turn don't count. A colleague at work has no limit. One waiting on a person has none either: its card has its own 24-hour deadline (`QUESTION_TTL_MS`, `APPROVAL_TTL_MS`), after which the run goes on and answers.
- **The row's time** runs from the ask to what came back, waits included. It reads in hours past the first, and "levou 6 h" once closed.

## Addendum (2026-11-10): one row for an exchange

A few questions and notes between the same two agents within minutes drew one row per asking run, back to back ("Ada ⇄ Theo · levou 4 min", then "Ada ⇄ Theo · levou 1 min"). Now questions and hand-offs between the same two agents, either way, that overlap or start within `EXCHANGE_GAP_MS` (5 minutes) of the last one's end are one exchange (`asExchanges` in `packages/client/src/lib/consult-group.ts`): its row draws on the reply of its first asking run, shows the other agent's face and the whole exchange's time, from the first ask to the last answer, and opens one conversation with all of its parts (`useExchangeConversation`). The web and the phone share it. The other runs' replies draw no row, and an answer's notice far from that row is the one line leading to it.
